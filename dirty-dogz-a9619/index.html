About us

Why pets love Dirty Dogz

Dirty Dogz was set up by Louise Judge to bring professional canine grooming to the Chichester area. Louise started out grooming her own Sussex Spaniels, Henry and Isabelle, and it quickly became clear she had a real talent for styling. She has trained on a number of grooming courses, including the NPTC Intermediate Certificate at Brinsbury, and was selected to compete at the European Grooming Championships in 2007. Every dog is handled patiently and groomed to suit its breed and its owner.

— The team at Dirty Dogz

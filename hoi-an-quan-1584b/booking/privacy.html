How your booking details are used

When you book online, the business you're booking with gets your name, phone number, email address, any note you add, and the time you booked. They use these only to look after your booking: to confirm it, to contact you about it if something changes, and to keep their diary.

The business is responsible for your details. Builds by Walden runs this booking system for them and only handles your details on their behalf, to make the booking system work.

What happens to your details

  • We email you a confirmation, with a link to cancel. The business gets an email about your booking.
  • Your details are stored securely with Google, which hosts the booking system and sends the emails.
  • We don't sell your details, share them with anyone else, or use them for marketing.
  • Bookings are deleted automatically 13 months after the booking date.

Your rights

You can ask the business to see, correct or delete your details. You can also contact Builds by Walden at jay@buildsbywalden.co.uk. If you're unhappy with how your details have been handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

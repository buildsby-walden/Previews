The small print, in plain English
Paying upfront
The monthly plan has no minimum term: stop it any time with 30 days' notice by email. The set-up fee isn't refundable once work has started.
Nothing upfront
A minimum of 12 months. After that it carries on month to month, and either of us can end it with 30 days' notice. To leave early, you either pay the months left up to month 12, or buy it outright for the upfront price less what you've already paid.
Every month
Your web address registered in your business's name, hosting with the padlock (HTTPS), fixing anything that stops working, and small changes when you ask (up to 30 minutes a month, such as new hours, prices or photos).
Anything bigger
New pages or new tools are quoted before I start. Nothing extra is charged without you agreeing it first.
Who owns what
Your web address, photos, logo and wording are yours. The design becomes yours once the set-up fee is paid, or after 12 monthly payments on the nothing-upfront plan.
If it ends
You get a copy of your website's files (once it's paid for), I help move your web address for free, and the site comes offline at the end of the notice period unless it has moved.
Price changes
At most once a year, with 60 days' notice, and never in the first 12 months of the nothing-upfront plan.
Customer details
Enquiries and bookings that come in are yours. I only handle them to run it, and keep them secure.
No promises
I'll build and run it with care, but nobody can promise more customers or a place in Google.

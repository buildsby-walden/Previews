How the booking system works for Hartlane School of Motoring Luton

  1. Your pupils choose from Beginner lessons (1, 1.5 or 2 hours), Pass Plus & motorway, Automatic lessons and Mock tests, then a day and a free time.
  2. They can choose Salman, Mark or Runa, or any instructor. Each instructor has their own diary, so one being booked doesn't stop pupils booking the others.
  3. They can book the same time every week, for up to 10 weeks, in one go. Any week that isn't free is named before they book.
  4. Times follow your opening hours on Google. In the demo, bookings must be made at least 1 day ahead, and 15 minutes are kept free between lessons for travel (you'd set your own rules).
  5. They add their name, number and email, and answer two quick questions: “Pick-up address or postcode” and “Driving so far”.
  6. It arrives as a request. You accept or decline it from your phone, and they get an email either way. The time is held until you answer.
  7. Your side works on your phone: your diary, adding phone bookings, time off, and changing your lessons, prices and hours yourself.
